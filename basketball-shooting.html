How to play

Press and drag on the ball toward the lower-left to charge, then release to shoot. The farther you drag, the more power. Score by putting the ball through the hoop.

Swish bonus

If the ball passes through the hoop without touching the rim or backboard, it counts as a swish and gives you extra points. Streaks also boost your score.

Play anywhere

Works with touch on mobile and mouse on desktop. Tap the fullscreen button in the corner for a bigger view. No download, no sign-up.

How to Play Basketball Shooting Game

  1. Drag to charge. Press and hold anywhere on the ball, then drag toward the lower-left. A power meter appears around the ball as you drag.
  2. Release to shoot. When you let go, the ball launches in the opposite direction of your drag. The angle of your drag controls the arc, and the distance controls the speed.
  3. Aim for the hoop. The hoop slowly drifts to a new position each round, and a small random wind pushes the ball sideways. Watch the wind indicator and adjust your aim.
  4. Score and swish. A made basket is 2 points. A swish — no contact with the rim or backboard — adds 1 extra point.
  5. Build a streak. Consecutive makes increase your streak, and each streak level adds up to 3 bonus points per shot.
  6. Auto reset. When the ball stops moving or leaves the screen, the next round begins automatically with a new hoop position and new wind.

FAQ

What is Basketball Shooting Game?

Basketball Shooting Game is a free side-view arcade basketball game that runs in your web browser. You drag the ball to charge a shot, release to shoot, and try to score as many baskets as possible. It works on both mobile and desktop with no download or sign-up.

How do I play Basketball Shooting Game?

Press and hold on the basketball, drag toward the lower-left to charge power, then release to shoot. The ball flies in the opposite direction of your drag. Aim so the ball passes through the hoop from above. Drag farther for more power.

How does scoring work in Basketball Shooting Game?

A normal basket is worth 2 points. A swish — when the ball goes through the hoop without touching the rim or backboard — adds 1 bonus point. Consecutive makes build a streak that adds up to 3 extra points per shot.

What is a swish in this basketball game?

A swish means the ball passes cleanly through the hoop without touching the rim or the backboard. In this game, a swish gives you bonus points and a special visual and sound effect.

Can I play Basketball Shooting Game on mobile?

Yes. Basketball Shooting Game supports touch controls on phones and tablets as well as mouse controls on desktop. The layout adapts to any screen size, and you can switch to full screen using the button in the bottom-right corner.

Is Basketball Shooting Game free to play?

Yes, it is completely free. There is no paywall, no account, and no download. The game runs entirely in your browser and does not upload any of your data.

Does the wind affect my shot?

Yes. Each round has a small random wind that pushes the ball left or right. The wind direction and strength are shown on screen, so you can adjust your aim slightly to compensate.

Can I play Basketball Shooting Game offline?

Yes, once the page and its game script are loaded, all gameplay logic runs locally in your browser. No internet connection is needed during play, and no data is sent to a server.
